Privacy Policy

Vibe Check: Dog Judge · Last updated 27 August 2026

Vibe Check is a comedy app. A cartoon dog rates a photo of you out of 100 and makes a joke about it. This policy describes exactly what happens to that photo.

What stays on your phone

Every vibe check you take is saved on your device: the photo, the score, and the caption. This is private to you. If you never post, your photos never leave your iPhone.

What we send, and when

To score a photo we send that image to our server, which forwards it to an AI provider (OpenRouter, using a Google Gemini model) to generate a score and a one-line caption. The image is used only to produce that result. We do not attach your name or any identifier to it.

If you tap Post to leaderboard, and only then, the photo, the score, the caption, and the display name you chose are stored on our server and shown publicly on the in-app leaderboard.

Names and sign-in

You may sign in with Apple, or simply type a display name. Sign in with Apple gives us an opaque Apple identifier and, on first sign-in only, your name. We keep these on your device. No account is created on our server. The only thing attached to a public post is the display name you picked.

Deleting your data

Go to You → Delete my account and all my data. This removes your posts and their photos from the leaderboard server, then erases your local vibe checks and sign-in. It cannot be undone. Deleting the app alone removes only the local copy.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics SDK, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale or sharing of personal data, and no location, contacts, or microphone access. The camera is used only when you take a vibe check.

Children

Vibe Check is not directed at children under 13.
