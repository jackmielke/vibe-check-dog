Vibe Check - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Vibe Check is a playful entertainment app with a public community leaderboard. This policy explains exactly what is and isn't shared.

Photo analysis and public posting

When you take or choose a photo for a vibe check, a resized copy is sent through our Supabase service to OpenRouter (Google Gemini) for automated analysis. The result includes a score, factor breakdown, and playful caption. The analyzed photo is not added to the public leaderboard unless you choose Post to Board.

If you post, the photo, chosen title, score, generated caption, and account identifier are stored by our service and become publicly visible in Vibe Check. Only post photos of yourself or people who agreed to be shared. Before public storage, a reduced copy is sent through our Supabase service to OpenRouter (Google Gemini) for a fail-closed safety check. If the check flags unsafe material or is unavailable, no public post is created.

Face data

What is collected. When you take or choose a photo for a vibe check, the app runs Apple's on-device Vision framework to detect whether a face is present and to estimate a few expression cues (smile, open eyes, framing, head pose, capture quality). These are turned into numeric scores between 0 and 100 (for example "Smile 72"). The app does not create a face template, faceprint, or any other biometric identifier, does not identify or recognize who is in the photo, and does not compare faces across photos or users.

Use. Face detection and expression cues are used only to produce the playful vibe score, factor breakdown, and caption you see. They are not used for authentication, advertising, profiling, tracking, or any other purpose.

Storage and retention. Face landmarks and expression measurements exist only in memory on your device while a score is computed and are discarded immediately afterward. They are never written to disk and never sent to our servers. Only the resulting numeric scores are kept, alongside the photo, in your private on-device history, until you delete the entry or the app. If you choose Post to Board, the photo and those numeric scores are stored by our service as part of your public post until you delete your account, remove the post, or moderation removes it.

Sharing. No face landmarks or expression measurements are shared with anyone. The resized photo itself is sent to OpenRouter (Google Gemini) for automated scoring and, if you post, for a safety check, as described above; our service does not extract or store face geometry from it, and does not keep a copy of the photo after returning the result. Face data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with data brokers.

What we collect

  • Photos: a resized copy is processed to create each score. Photos you post are stored on the public leaderboard until you remove your account or we remove the content.
  • User content: titles, comments, generated captions, scores, reports, blocks, and feedback you choose to submit.
  • Name and account identifier: posting and commenting require Sign in with Apple. Our service privately uses Apple's stable app-specific identifier to authenticate account actions. Public posts and comments contain a separate opaque identifier so content from one account can be grouped, followed, blocked, and deleted without publishing the Apple identifier or email address.
  • Email address: if Apple provides a verified email or private relay address, our service may retain it privately as an account alias so legacy posts can be linked to the correct account and removed during account deletion. Signed-in feedback is associated with the stable account identifier, not the email address.

We do not collect your contacts or location, we show no advertising, and we do not track you or use third-party advertising analytics.

Supabase provides database, storage, and edge-function infrastructure. Vibe Check's Edge Functions do not intentionally keep separate copies of scoring or safety requests after returning a result. OpenRouter (Google Gemini) processes the resized image used for automated scoring and public-post safety. OpenRouter and Google may process or log submitted content for security, service operation, and product improvement under its API terms and privacy policy, which govern OpenRouter's retention. If scoring is unavailable, the app falls back to on-device image analysis. Public posts are stored separately by Supabase until account deletion, moderation removal, or service cleanup.

Account deletion

Open Profile, then choose Delete account and confirm with Sign in with Apple. The app deletes public posts and stored photos associated with your verified Apple identity, comments attributable to you, your moderation records and identifiable feedback, invites you created, and redemption details on invites you used. It then removes your local sign-in, private history, and profile photo. If the server deletion cannot finish, the app keeps your account signed in and asks you to try again.

The deletion service exchanges the fresh Apple authorization code and revokes the resulting Sign in with Apple token before it reports success. It also invalidates existing Vibe Check sessions. You can contact us for help with a deletion request.

Moderation & your controls

You can report any public post or comment. It is hidden from you immediately and the report is reviewed within 24 hours. You can also block an identified community member to hide that member's public posts and comments. Objectionable content and abusive users are not tolerated.

Local data

We use UserDefaults for your local identity, profile settings, preferences, blocked list, reactions, and community-guideline agreement. Your private vibe history and profile photo are stored in the app's local container.

Children's privacy

Vibe Check is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13.
