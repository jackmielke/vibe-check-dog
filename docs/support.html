What is Vibe Check?

Take a photo or choose one from your library to get a playful vibe score from 0 to 100 with a witty breakdown. Keep results in your private history, share a result card, or post your best vibes to the public worldwide leaderboard. It is all just for fun.

Frequently asked questions

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?
A resized copy is sent to our scoring service for analysis. It is not added to the public leaderboard unless you tap Post to Board and it passes the public-post safety check. If screening is unavailable, no public post is created. Public posts remain visible until your account is deleted or the content is removed.

The camera won't start.
Make sure Vibe Check has camera permission in Settings → Vibe Check → Camera. You can also tap "Choose from Library" to score any photo instead.

How do I delete a saved result?
Open Profile, select a result in Device Vibe History, and tap Delete.

How do I delete my account?
Open Profile, find the account section, tap Delete account, then confirm with Sign in with Apple. The app removes public posts and stored photos associated with your verified Apple identity, attributable comments and reports, identifiable feedback, and your invite or redemption data. It then clears your local sign-in, private history, and profile photo. If server deletion fails, nothing local is cleared and you can retry.

This process revokes the Sign in with Apple authorization token obtained during confirmation and invalidates existing Vibe Check sessions before it reports success.

Is the score real?
It is a lighthearted entertainment score generated from visual cues. Take it in good fun!

How do reporting and blocking work?
Long-press a leaderboard post, or open it, to report objectionable content. Each comment also has a menu for reporting it or blocking its identified author. Reports hide that item immediately and are reviewed within 24 hours. Blocking hides that member's public posts and comments.
