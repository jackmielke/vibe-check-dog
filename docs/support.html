Vibe Check: Dog Judge

Open the app, take a photo, and a very unimpressed dog scores your vibe out of 100 and roasts you in one line.

Questions

How do I get a vibe check?
Tap Take my vibe check on the Today tab, allow the camera, and press the shutter.
Is the score real?
No. It is a joke generated by an AI model. It means nothing.
Do my photos go anywhere?
A photo is sent to be scored, then stays on your phone unless you tap Post to leaderboard.
Can I change who judges me?
Yes. You → Who judges you switches between The Dog, Cartoon Dog, Chill Dog, and The Critic. Each scores on different criteria.
How do I delete my posts and account?
You → Delete my account and all my data. It removes your leaderboard posts and photos from the server and wipes local data.
Something on the leaderboard is offensive.
Press and hold the entry, then choose Hide this post or Report. Reporting hides it immediately and emails us.
Turn off the sounds.
You → Sound effects.
