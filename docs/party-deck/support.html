What is Party Deck?

One phone, everyone plays. Pick a deck, put the phone on your forehead, and your friends describe the card while you guess. Tilt down when you get it, tilt up to pass. Make your own decks or let the AI write one from any topic.

Frequently asked questions

The tilt is not registering.
Hold the phone flat against your forehead, screen facing out, and tilt the top of the phone clearly down (correct) or up (pass). The two buttons at the bottom of the card do the same thing if you would rather tap.

AI deck says it is unavailable.
It needs an internet connection, and there is a limit of about 20 generated decks per hour per network. Custom decks you type yourself never need a connection.

How do I edit or delete a deck I made?
Press and hold the deck on the home screen.
