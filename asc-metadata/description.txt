A very unimpressed dog looks you in the face and rates your vibe out of 100.

Open the app, take a photo, and wait. The dog scores you, then says one short, specific thing about what it sees. Not a horoscope, not a compliment generator. It notices the necklace, the hoodie you have worn three days running, the fact that you clearly just woke up.

CHOOSE WHO JUDGES YOU
Each character scores on completely different criteria, so the same photo gets very different verdicts.
- The Dog: how together you look
- Cartoon Dog: same judge, drawn
- Chill Dog: only cares how relaxed you are. Looking busy costs you points
- The Critic: judges your selfie as though it were hanging in a gallery

THE LEADERBOARD
See how everyone else did. Your vibe checks stay on your phone until you choose to post one, and posting is entirely optional. Tap any entry to see it full size.

KEEP A RECORD
Every check is saved locally with its score and caption, so you can watch your average, your best day, and your streak.

Vibe Check is a joke. The dog is not a real judge of anything, and the scores mean nothing at all.
