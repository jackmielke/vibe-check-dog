WHAT THIS APP IS
A comedy app. The user takes a photo of themselves, an AI model scores it 0-100 and writes one
short joke line about it, and the user can optionally post that result to a public leaderboard.
Target audience: general, 17+. The value is a quick shareable laugh and a light daily habit.

HOW TO USE IT (no account or credentials required)
1. Launch. The Today tab shows a dog and a "Take my vibe check" button.
2. Tap it and allow camera access. An in-app camera opens, front-facing.
3. Press the white shutter. A score, emoji, and caption appear in a few seconds. THIS WORKS
   WITHOUT SIGNING IN and the result stays only on the device.
4. Leaderboard tab: public posts from all users, sortable by Top vibes or Most recent. Tap any
   row for a full-size detail view.
5. You tab: optional sign-in, character picker, statistics, and data deletion.
NO DEMO ACCOUNT IS NEEDED. Every core feature except posting works signed out.

SIGN-IN AND ACCOUNT DELETION
Sign in with Apple is optional and is used only to attach a display name to a public post. A user
can instead type any display name. No account is created on our server; the Apple identifier
stays on device. Account deletion is in the app at You -> "Delete my account and all my data".
It calls a server endpoint that deletes the user's leaderboard posts and their stored photos,
then erases local data. It is reachable without contacting support.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT AND MODERATION
The leaderboard is user-submitted photos, so there are three layers:
1. Pre-publication screening. Every photo passes an automated safety classifier before it can be
   stored or posted. Nudity or sexual content, apparent minors in inappropriate contexts, graphic
   violence, and hate symbols are rejected and never reach storage or the leaderboard.
2. In-app reporting and hiding. Press and hold any entry for "Hide this post" (immediate and
   permanent for that user) or "Report" (hides it and emails the developer the post id).
3. Server-side takedown via an is_hidden flag, which drops a row from the public feed in seconds
   with no app update.
Contact for reports and takedowns is published in the app, in the support page, and in the
privacy policy: jackcmielke@gmail.com

EXTERNAL SERVICES USED
- Supabase (database, storage, serverless functions) hosts the leaderboard and photos.
- OpenRouter, calling a Google Gemini vision model, produces the score, caption, and safety
  verdict. The API key is server-side and never in the app binary.
- Sign in with Apple, for the optional display name only.
No analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or payment SDKs.

PERMISSIONS
Camera only, requested at the moment the user taps "Take my vibe check", with the purpose string
"Vibe Check needs the camera so the dog can look you in the face and rate your vibe." No location,
contacts, microphone, photo library, notifications, or App Tracking Transparency prompts.
